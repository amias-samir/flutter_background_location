# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Flutter Background Location Tracker is an open-source project where people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience can learn, contribute, and work
together. We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, inclusive,
and professional community for everyone.

By participating, we ask contributors and maintainers to treat one another with
kindness, patience, and consideration. Healthy technical discussion and
different points of view are welcome when they remain constructive and focused
on improving the project.

## How we work together

We encourage community members to:

- Communicate clearly, respectfully, and with empathy.
- Welcome questions and help people who are new to the project.
- Give constructive feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Be open to different approaches, experiences, and perspectives.
- Acknowledge mistakes, make reasonable efforts to correct them, and learn from
  the experience.
- Assume good intent while remaining mindful of how words and actions may affect
  others.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its community.

Behavior that does not support a healthy community includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or unwanted personal attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, deliberately disruptive comments, or repeated
  hostile communication.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or conduct.
- Sharing another person's private information without clear permission.
- Publishing precise locations, route histories, device identifiers, diagnostic
  data, or other sensitive information without the owner's consent.
- Any behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional and collaborative environment.

Disagreement is a normal part of open-source work. Contributors are welcome to
challenge ideas and technical decisions, provided the conversation remains
respectful, evidence-based, and directed toward a useful outcome.

## Privacy and location data

This project works with potentially sensitive location information. Before
posting logs, screenshots, sample databases, GPX, KML, or GeoJSON files, please
remove real coordinates, route names, device identifiers, and personal details.
When possible, use synthetic or intentionally anonymized test data.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and other official communication channels. It also
applies when someone is publicly representing the project or its community.

## When concerns arise

Most misunderstandings can be resolved through calm and respectful discussion.
If that is not appropriate, or if you experience or observe concerning behavior,
please report it privately to the project maintainer at
[samirdangal@gmail.com](mailto:samirdangal@gmail.com).

Please do not include private location data or sensitive personal information
in a public issue. Reports will be considered carefully, handled as
confidentially as practical, and reviewed without retaliation against someone
who raises a concern in good faith.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for applying these standards fairly and
consistently. Depending on the context and impact, a maintainer may clarify an
expectation, request an edit or apology, moderate content, issue a private
warning, limit participation temporarily, or restrict participation
permanently.

The response should be proportionate to the situation, prioritize the safety
and well-being of the community, and allow room for learning and correction
when appropriate. Reasons for moderation decisions will be communicated when
doing so is practical and does not compromise anyone's privacy or safety.
